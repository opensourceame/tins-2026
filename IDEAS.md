

* when two alien species combine with complimentary traits they increase their chances of escaping
* when alien species conflict they can wipe each other out
* send capturing moons out to different solar systems to capture aliens
* the “zoo” is as described in the book, multiple tubes that extend from a central point - aliens are shunted into the tubes when captured


Skills required to play:

* timing - species die out
* placement - ensure complimentary trait species aren’t placed next to each other


Species traits

* reckless
* persuasive
* intelligence
* physical traits?  Heavy G makes physically stronger creatures.
* healing - helps other species survive


Spica

* a sphere (circle in 2d) which allows for bits to be attached
* constantly rotating
* can attach components - habitats, detector dish, trap launcher


DetectorDish

Detects signals from advancing species (e.g. TV / radio waves)



Game mechanics

* place bars / tubes on the “zoo”.
* placement of capturing moon determines race intelligence.  The further away from the home planet the more advanced the species needs
* zoom out to see worlds to send probes to
