# Game Objective

Keep your zoo running for as long as possible.

# Gameplay

You are the ruler of the artifical planet Spica, constructed next to a massive quasar, which is the
home of the largest galactic zoo in the galaxy.

## Spica Core

The planet is designed to be modular and house different modules, namely:


### Detector Dish

A massive dish that can detect intelligent life from emitted radio waves.  The dish can be upgraded
to detect life from greater and greater distances.

### Trap Launcher

The trap launcher launches moon traps.

#### Moon Traps

Moon traps are as the name suggests - a trap that looks like a moon.  When intelligent species visit
what they think is a moon they are whipped back to Spica to be added to the zoo's collection.

### Habitats

Habitats are where the zoo vicims live.  A habitat can be grown as more species are captured.
Habitats can become overcrowded if a moon-trap brings back more members of the species than there
is capacity for.  Species can also reproduce in captivity, causing population pressure.

Zoo captives need a habitat that mimics the conditions of their home planet.  There are four types of
environment:

#### Oxygen

These house species like humans and other air-breathing creatures.

### Water

These house creatures that survive in water

#### Sulphuric

A sulphuric atmoshphere is the home of some strange looking creatures

#### Plasma

Plasma creatures do not conform to any specific shape.

### Energy Collector

The energy collector mounted to Spica absorbs neutrino energy from the nearby quasar.  Upgrading the collector
makes the energy conversion more efficient.

### Repair module

Spica can be damaged, by a returning moon trap crashing into it (when it cannot find a habitat to house
its victims, for example).  When Spica takes damage the energy slowly drains from the planet, thus a
repair module should be installed to repair the damage and stop energy leakage.

Once repairs are completed a repair module will disassemble.

## Important to know

The location of planets in a solar system is important.

If you send a moon trap to a solar system it will get sucked into the orbit of the first
planet with intelligent life that it encounters.  This means you could bring back a different
species to the one you intended.  Timing is important.

## Visitors

Visitors are allowed at the zoo all day, every day.  However Spican zoo visitors are notoriously
fickle.  They like to see a variety of species.  Be sure to capture as many different species as
possible to keep them interested.

## Game ending

If you run out of energy, or visitors stop visiting due to lack of interest, the zoo will close.

# Input

Use the mouse to click on things to build in the left menu.
Click a planet to launch a moon trap at the planet.
You can change the game time using the keys 1, 2 and 3
Press R to restart the game
Press S to save, L to load
Press G to to to Game Over screen

### For testing

When you have a water habitat you can press W multiple times to generate overcrowded habitat segments until the habitat collapses.
